Figure 3.1: NEC vs NEA — the regulator and the association

The Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) is an autonomous statutory body under the NEC Act 2055 BS, headed by a Chairperson appointed by the Government of Nepal, with the Registrar as Member Secretary and a 4-year council tenure. The Nepal Engineers’ Association (NEA) is a voluntary professional association promoting welfare, networking and advocacy — with no legal enforcement power.

NEC jurisdiction: all engineering branches (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Computer, Electronics, Architecture, etc.), both government and private engineers, foreign engineers practising in Nepal, and engineering consultancy firms.

NEC duties/functions: register engineers; grant, renew, suspend or cancel licences; formulate the code of conduct; take disciplinary action; accredit degree programmes; set minimum education standards; advise government; fix fees; and maintain the register of licensed engineers.

Professional practice in Nepal is regulated by a stack of Acts, Rules and technical codes.

InstrumentPurpose
NEC Act 2055Formation of NEC; registration, licensing, code of conduct, discipline, accreditation.
Building Act 2055 → Building Rules 2062Regulate construction against earthquake, fire and natural calamity.
National Building Code (NBC) 2060Technical design standards (e.g. NBC 105 Seismic Design, NBC 114 Construction Safety).
Electronic Transactions Act (ETA) 2063Cyber law: electronic records, digital signatures, computer-crime punishment, IT Tribunal.

NBC Building Classification

  • International State-of-Art (NBC 000) — highest, for special structures.
  • Professionally Engineered (NBC 101–114) — >1000 sq.ft, >3 storeys, span >4.5 m.
  • Mandatory Rules of Thumb (MRT) (NBC 201/202/205) — <1000 sq.ft, ≤3 storeys.
  • Remote Rural Guidelines (NBC 203/204) — ≤2 storeys.
Building bylaws control land use through ROW (Right of Way), Set Back, FAR (floor area ÷ plot area), GCR (ground-floor area ÷ plot area) and height limits. Building permission from the municipality is mandatory before construction.

Why Practice Requires a Licence

  • Public safety — engineered structures directly affect lives.
  • Quality assurance — guarantees a minimum level of competence.
  • Accountability — registered engineers answer to the NEC.
  • Legal requirement — NEC Act 2055 makes unlicensed practice illegal and punishable.
  • Consumer protection — verifiable credentials and a complaints channel.
Disciplinary powers: the NEC can grant, renew, suspend or cancel a licence. For workplace misconduct the Labour Act 2074 sets a due-process ladder: Warning → Suspension → Termination, each with an opportunity to explain, investigation and hearing.

Contract Essentials

Contract
An agreement enforceable by law. Every contract is an agreement, but not every agreement is a contract.

Nine essential elements: offer & acceptance, competent parties, mutual intent, consideration, capacity (16+, sound mind), lawful purpose, free consent, possibility of performance and certainty of terms.

Void vs voidable: a void contract has no legal effect from inception (e.g. minor, unlawful object, impossibility, uncertainty, restraint of trade/marriage, wager). A voidable contract is valid until the aggrieved party cancels it (grounds: coercion, undue influence, fraud, misrepresentation).

Types of construction contract: Unit Price/Item Rate, Lump Sum, Cost-Plus (cost reimbursable), Time & Material, Design-Build, Management, Performance-Based Maintenance and Percentage-wise.

Public Procurement Act 2063 — Six Methods

Figure 3.3: Six Procurement Methods under PPA 2063

Consultant recruitment: EOI (Expression of Interest) shortlists qualified firms, then RFP (Request for Proposal) with a full Terms of Reference invites technical and financial proposals. Pre-qualification applies to contracts > NRs 60 lakh.

Tender notice must state: procuring entity, project description/location, estimated cost, publication date, where to obtain bid documents and their cost, submission deadline, bid-opening details, bid-security amount/form, eligibility and the right to accept/reject any bid.

Financial safeguards: Bid Security (Earnest Money) = 2–3% of the estimate; Performance Security = 5% of the contract (10% for foreign). Bid validity is always shorter than bid-security validity. Document priority: Contract Agreement > Letter of Acceptance > Bid > SCC > GCC > Specifications > Drawings > BOQ.

The Labour Act 2074 governs the rights, facilities and safety of workers. An enterprise is a firm with 10 or more staff.

Worker Death at Site — Procedure

Stop work and secure the area; give first aid; notify police, the Labour Office, management and family; document the scene (photos, witnesses, report); fulfil legal obligations under the Labour Act 2074 (compensation, accident insurance, funeral expenses, cooperate with investigation); and carry out an internal investigation with corrective safety measures and training before resuming.

Intellectual Property (IP)
Creations of the mind — inventions, literary/artistic works, designs, symbols, names and images used in commerce — over which the owner holds an exclusive legal right.

Why IPR is necessary: it encourages innovation, protects creators, drives economic development and technology transfer, protects consumers and supports international trade (Nepal is a member of WIPO and WTO).

TypeProtectsTermGoverning Act
CopyrightLiterary/artistic worksLife + 50 yearsCopyright Act 2059
PatentNew invention/process7 yrs + 2 renewals = 21Patent, Design & Trademark Act 2022
Industrial DesignShape/pattern/ornament5 yrs + 2 renewals = 15Patent, Design & Trademark Act 2022
TrademarkMark for goods7 yrs, renewable indefinitelyPatent, Design & Trademark Act 2022

A patent must be novel, non-obvious and industrially applicable. A service mark is like a trademark but identifies services (relevant to engineering consultancy firms). Copyright violation carries penalties of NRs 10,000–1,00,000 and/or 6 months.

Arbitration

Arbitration is the binding resolution of a dispute by an arbitrator or panel, governed by the Arbitration Act 2055. It is preferred over litigation for its speed, confidentiality, technical expertise, procedural flexibility and finality. For public projects (PPA 2063 / PPR 2064): contracts ≤ NRs 100 million use a Sole Adjudicator; those > NRs 100 million use a 3-member Dispute Review Board (DRB).

Conflict
A disagreement or clash arising from differences in interests, values, goals, perceptions or resource allocation — natural and often unavoidable in complex, multi-stakeholder projects.

Three views: Traditional (conflict is harmful → avoid), Behavioural (conflict is natural → accept and manage) and Interactionist (some conflict is beneficial → encourage healthy conflict). Types: functional/constructive vs dysfunctional/destructive. Levels: intrapersonal, interpersonal, intergroup and inter-organizational. Causes: scarce resources, ambiguous jurisdiction, communication breakdown, personality clashes and change.
