3.1 INTRODUCTION & CLASSIFICATION

A column is a compression member whose effective length exceeds 3 times its least lateral dimension, carrying primarily axial load (often with moments). Classification:

  • By slenderness: short if both slenderness ratios lexD and leyb < 12, otherwise long / slender.
  • By loading: axially loaded; axial + uniaxial moment; axial + biaxial moment.
  • By lateral reinforcement: tied or spirally reinforced.
  • By shape: rectangular, square, circular, etc.
Minimum eccentricity (IS 456): emin = l500 + D30 ≥ 20 mm. If the actual eccentricity does not exceed 0.05D, the column may be designed as axially loaded.

3.3 SHORT AXIALLY-LOADED COLUMNS

Ultimate axial capacity (short tied column)
Pu = 0.4·fck·Ac + 0.67·fy·Asc
(Ac = net concrete area; Asc = area of longitudinal steel.) Includes an allowance for the minimum eccentricity up to 0.05D.

For spirally reinforced short columns, the capacity may be increased by 5%: Pu = 1.05(0.4fckAc + 0.67fyAsc), provided the spiral satisfies the volumetric ratio requirement.

3.4 INTERACTION DIAGRAMS

An interaction diagram is the locus of all (Pu, Mu) combinations that cause failure of a given column section — a plot of axial capacity vs moment capacity.

Figure 3.1: Column interaction diagram — above the balanced point failure is by concrete crushing (compression), below it by steel yielding (tension)

Design charts in SP-16 plot PufckbD against MufckbD² for various pfck and d′D, allowing direct selection of the steel ratio p = 100AscbD.

3.5 SHORT COLUMNS WITH UNIAXIAL BENDING

  1. Compute factored Pu and Mu (include minimum eccentricity).
  2. Assume a section (b×D) and d′D; compute PufckbD and MufckbD².
  3. From the SP-16 chart, read pfck → steel p = (pfck)×fck → Asc = pbD100.
  4. Provide bars (≥ 0.8% and ≤ 6% of gross area); design ties.

3.6 SHORT COLUMNS WITH BIAXIAL BENDING

Biaxial interaction (IS 456 cl. 39.6)
(MuxMux1)αn + (MuyMuy1)αn ≤ 1.0

Mux1, Muy1 are the uniaxial moment capacities about each axis for the given Pu (from SP-16). The exponent αn varies from 1.0 to 2.0 with PuPuz (0.2 → 1.0, 0.8 → 2.0), where Puz = 0.45fckAc + 0.75fyAsc. Iterate the steel until the interaction check ≤ 1.0.

3.7 LONG (SLENDER) COLUMNS

When leD or leb ≥ 12, the column is slender and additional moments from lateral deflection must be added:

Additional moment: Max = PuD(lexD)²2000 and May = Pub(leyb)²2000 (IS 456 cl. 39.7), multiplied by a reduction factor k = Puz − PuPuz − Pb ≤ 1. These are added to the primary moments before designing as a column with bending.

3.8 REINFORCEMENT DETAILING

  • Longitudinal steel: minimum 0.8%, maximum 6% of gross area (4% practical); minimum 4 bars (rectangular), 6 bars (circular); bar diameter ≥ 12 mm.
  • Ties (lateral): diameter ≥ ¼ of the largest longitudinal bar and ≥ 6 mm; spacing ≤ least lateral dimension, 16× smallest longitudinal bar diameter, and 300 mm.
  • Cover: ≥ 40 mm (or bar diameter).
  • Every corner and alternate bar restrained by a tie corner; no bar farther than 150 mm from a restrained bar.
