A slab is one-way when the longer-to-shorter clear span ratio lylx > 2 (it bends essentially in one direction); otherwise it is two-way and bends in both directions. Almost all IOE questions are two-way restrained panels with various edge continuities.

Design method (two-way restrained slab)

  1. Depth: pick D from lxd ≈ 28–32 (continuous), then d = D − cover − φ/2.
  2. Loads: w = 1.5(DL + FF + LL); DL = 25×D kN/m² per metre width.
  3. Moments: Mx = αxw·lx², My = αyw·lx² using the IS 456 Table 26 coefficients for the given edge condition (separate −ve support and +ve mid-span values).
  4. Steel: Ast = 0.5fckfy[1 − √(1 − 4.6Mufckbd²)]bd ≥ 0.12% bD (min); spacing ≤ 3d or 300 mm.
  5. Checks: shear (τv ≤ k·τc, usually safe), deflection (span/d with modification factor), development length at supports.
  6. Torsion steel at corners where the slab is discontinuous and held down = ¾ of the mid-span steel, over lx5 in each direction, in the top and bottom.

Typical result: for a 5×4 m two-way panel the design steel is at or near the 0.12% minimum — nominal 8 mm bars at 120–300 mm c/c both ways — and the deflection (span/d) check usually governs the slab thickness rather than the moment.

7.3 Design & Detailing of Longitudinally Loaded Stairs 207420682056

PAST EXAM QUESTIONS:

  1. Concept of design of a staircase. Detailing of a straight flight in plan and section. [5] (2056 Bhadra, Q3a)
  2. Design and detailing of a RCC staircase. Define the effective span for a staircase. [5+1] (2074 Ashwin, Q4b)
  3. Explain the detailing of reinforcement in staircases. [5] (2068 Baishakh, Q3a)

A longitudinally-spanning stair (flight spanning between supports at the two landings) is designed as a one-way inclined slab of width equal to the flight. The waist slab carries the load; the steps are non-structural.

  • Effective span = the going + half of each landing (or the centre-to-centre of supports); IS 456 gives specific rules depending on how the landing spans.
  • Loads (on plan, per metre width): self-weight of the waist (25×t·√(R²+T²)T for the slope), steps (½×25×R), finishes and LL (3–5 kN/m²). The landing carries a UDL over its length.
  • Design: M = wl²8 (SS) → main bars along the span in the bottom of the waist; distribution steel across.

Detailing: main bars run along the flight in the bottom face; at the junction of flight and landing the tension bars are on the inside of the re-entrant corner (a bar detailed around the outside would tend to straighten and spall the cover). Provide distribution steel (0.12%) transversely and adequate anchorage of the main bars into the supports.
