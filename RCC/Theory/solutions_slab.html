Every past-exam question of this chapter is stated in full — with its exam sitting (year & session), question number and marks — and answered below.

Two-way restrained panel: main steel spans the short direction lx; torsion mesh is provided at corners that are held down; a discontinuous edge carries about half the mid-span steel as top steel.
Classify: lylx > 2 → one-way; ≤ 2 → two-way.
Depth: d = lxbasic Ld × MF, basic = 20 (SS), 26 (continuous), × ~1.3–1.5 modification.
Load: wu = 1.5(self-wt + floor finish + live load) per m².
One-way: Mu = wulx²8 (or IS 456 Table 12 coefficients); design a 1 m strip.
Two-way: Mx = αxwulx², My = αywulx² (Table 26 restrained / Table 27 simply supported).
Steel: Ast from Mu; min 0.12% (HYSD); spacing ≤ 3d, 300 mm. Distribution steel 0.12%. Corner torsion steel = ¾·mid-span steel over lx5 (restrained, corners held).

1.  Design a RC slab for an interior panel supported on RCC beams on all sides, clear dimensions 4×5 m, carrying superimposed live load 3 kN/m² and floor finish 2 kNm². Use M20 and Fe415, with the necessary checks and drawings. 2081 Baishakh · Q3a [15 marks]

lylx = 54 = 1.25 → two-way. Assume D = 150 mm, d = 125 mm. Self-wt = 0.15×25 = 3.75; wu = 1.5(3.75 + 2 + 3) = 13.13 kN/m².

Interior panel coefficients (Table 26, lylx = 1.25): αx = 0.037 (−ve), 0.028 (+ve); αy = 0.032 (−ve), 0.024 (+ve).

Mx = 0.037×13.13×4² = 7.77 kN-m (support), 5.88 (mid); My = 0.032×13.13×16 = 6.72 (support), 5.04 (mid).

Ast from Mu (per m): for Mx=7.77, Ast ≈ Mu0.87fy·0.9d ≈ 190 mm²/m < Ast,min = 0.12%×1000×150 = 180 mm² ⇒ nominal steel: 8 mm @ 200 mm c/c both ways. Provide corner torsion mesh; check deflection (Ld = 4000125 = 32 ≤ 26×MF).

Each panel: classify (lylx), find wu, read αxαy (Table 26 restrained / 27 simply supported) for the edge condition, compute MxMy, design a 1 m strip (D assumed, d = D−20). Distribution steel 0.12%; corner torsion mesh where corners are held; deflection (Ld) checked.

18.  Explain the design and detailing of a RCC (longitudinally spanning) staircase and define its effective span; show the detailing of a straight flight in plan and section. 2074 Ashwin · Q4b 2068 Baishakh · Q3a 2056 Bhadra · Q3a [5+1 marks]

A longitudinally spanning stair is designed as a one-way slab spanning between the supports (landings/beams). Steps:

(1) Effective span (IS 456 cl.33): the going plus half of each landing (or centre-to-centre of supports). (2) Loads on the horizontal plan: waist-slab self-weight (on the slope, projected to plan) + step self-weight (½·rise·γ) + floor finish + live load. (3) Mu = wuleff²8 → main steel along the span. (4) Distribution steel across the width (0.12%). (5) Detail so bars are anchored into the landings and the tension bars are kept on the correct face at the kink to avoid bursting.

Section of a longitudinally spanning flight: the inclined waist slab carries the main tension bars; the effective span runs between the landing supports.
