CHAPTER 9 — DESIGN OF FOOTINGS

Every past-exam question of this chapter is stated in full — with its exam sitting (year & session), question number and marks — and answered below.

Isolated footing: the net upward soil pressure bends the slab as a cantilever about the column face; punching shear is checked on the perimeter at d2 around the column.
1. Plan area A = 1.1P/SBC (service, +10% self-wt) → side L = √A.
2. Net factored upward pressure pu = 1.5PL×B.
3. Cantilever a = L − column2; bending at face Mu = pu·B·a²2.
4. Effective depth: flexure d = √(Mu0.138fckB); punching (perimeter d2, τv ≤ 0.25√fck) usually governs → adopt a larger D.
5. Ast from Mu (per m width); check one-way shear at d and development length.

1.  Design an isolated footing for a column 500×500 mm carrying a service axial load of 1600 kN. SBC 125 kN/m² at 1.25 m depth. Use Fe500 (TMT) and M25. Provide construction drawings. 2082 Bhadra · Q5a [14 marks]

A=1.1×1600125=14.1 m² → 3.8×3.8 m. pu=1.5×160014.44=166 kNm². a=3.8−0.52=1.65 m; Mu=166×3.8×1.65²2=860 kN-m. dflex=256; punching governs → D≈700 mm (dpunch=610).

2.  Design a footing for a square column 400×400 mm carrying a service load of 1500 kN. SBC 150 kNm². Use M20 and Fe415. 2081 Baishakh · Q4b [10 marks]

A=11.0 m² → 3.4×3.4 m. pu=195 kNm². a=1.5 m; Mu=745 kN-m. dflex=282; punching (dpunch=708) governs → D≈770 mm.

9.  Design a RC spread footing for a RC wall of 4 m length and 300 mm width carrying a total load of 1000 kN at service. Use M20 and Fe415, SBC 150 kNm². 2065 Kartik · Q4a [14 marks]

Load per m run=10004=250 kNm. Width B=1.1×250150=1.83 → 1.9 m. pu=1.5×2501.9=197 kNm². Cantilever a=1.9−0.32=0.8 m; Mu=197×0.8²2=63 kN-m/m; d≈150 mm → design 1 m strip (one-way footing).

10.  Design an isolated footing for a square column 400×400 mm carrying a service load of 1200 kN. SBC 150 kN/m²; unit weight 18 kNm³. Use M20 and Fe415. 2074 Chaitra · Q4a [10 marks]

A = 1.1×1200150 = 8.8 m² → 3.0 × 3.0 m. pu = 1.5×12009 = 200 kNm².

a = 3.0 − 0.42 = 1.3 m; Mu = 200×3.0×1.3²2 = 507 kN-m. dflexure = √(507×1060.138×20×3000) = 247 mm.

Punching (perimeter 4(400+d), τv ≤ 0.25√20 = 1.12) governs ⇒ adopt d ≈ 550–600, D ≈ 650 mm. Then Ast from Mu per metre, and check one-way shear at d from the face.

When the column transmits a moment M (e=MP): pressures pmax/min=(PLB)(1±6eL). Keep e≤L6 (no uplift); enlarge the base until pmax≤SBC, then design the critical strip for pmax.

Combined footing 2072 Kartik · Q1b [16] (rectangular footing, column load 1150 + BM 250 from 600 col, SBC 200): proportion the base so its centroid coincides with the resultant of the column loads → uniform pressure; analyse as a beam spanning the columns (longitudinal steel for sagging/hogging moments) and as a slab transversely.

Mat foundation 2081 Bhadra · Q5a 2075 Ashwin · Q4b 2069 Chaitra · Q3a (“describe design procedure”): check Σloads/area ≤ SBC; analyse by the conventional rigid method (inverted floor loaded by soil pressure, divided into strips) or the flexible (beam-on-elastic-foundation) method; design the slab for the moments and check punching at each column.

Punching shear in footings 2067 Ashadh · Q3b [8]: critical section at d2 from the column face; τv=Vperimeter·d ≤ ks·0.25√fck. If exceeded, increase depth (footings are not normally provided with shear reinforcement).
