i Course Information

Course: Design of RCC Structures (ENCE 352)  •  Year / Part: III / II

Credits: Lecture 4  •  Tutorial 2  •  Practical 2  •  11 Chapters • 60 Hours • 60 Marks

Objective: To equip students to design building elements using reinforced concrete by the limit state method — using structural analysis results, applying relevant code provisions, detailing reinforcement, and covering seismic-code requirements for ductile detailing.

Codes & References: IS 456:2000, IS 13920, IS 875, IS 1893, SP 16; Jain (Limit State Design), Pillai & Menon, Dayaratnam, Agrawal & Shrikhande.
